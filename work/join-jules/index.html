Join Jules

Join Jules, created by Julianna McIntosh, is the go-to destination for cocktail enthusiasts. With balanced sips, seasonal ingredients, and intriguing combinations, it’s all about creating the happiest of hours at home. Fresh off the launch of her recipe book, Julianna’s website needed a redesign to match her growing brand. The new site brings her retro, vibrant aesthetic to life.

Scope

  • User Experience
  • Content Strategy
  • Web Design
Website Link

Design Role

I worked alongside Do-Hee Kim to rebuild the blog experience from the ground up. Transitioning the site from Squarespace to WordPress, we focused on SEO, usability, and a scalable site architecture ready for every round she shakes up next.

Bold illustrations, punchy colors, and whimsical touches capture her joy and energy with a hint of the Italian coast. The result is a delightful, easy-to-navigate site that beautifully indexes her library of recipes, guides, and product picks.
