Ten Little

Ten Little makes it easy for parents to find healthy, expert-picked essentials for kids at every age, with personalized recommendations based on size and stage. Each product is vetted for quality, safety, and sustainability. From their 2020 debut to becoming a household name, they needed an embedded design team to evolve the site alongside them.

Scope

  • User Experience
  • Content Strategy
  • Web Design

Design Role

Alongside Do-Hee Kim at Shoppe Theory, we worked on their initial web launch back in early 2020 to create a website that captured their unique vision.

As they’ve grown into a household name across the country, we continued to work with on various user experience projects.

Most notably, we shaped the size and stage shopping experience at the heart of Ten Little, along with a personalized gifting flow and a donation directory.

Gifting

When it comes to giving gifts, we’re all looking to make things as easy and thoughtful as possible. Ten Little wanted the process to feel just as personal.

The result is a quick, intuitive flow that lets shoppers add custom physical gift notes to their orders.

Donation Directory

Kids outgrow things fast. Donating is one of the simplest ways to shrink your carbon footprint and give back, so Ten Little wanted to help parents find local places to pass along children’s goods.

The donation directory makes that search effortless, pairing helpful information with clear, straightforward navigation.
