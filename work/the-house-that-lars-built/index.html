The House That Lars Built

The House That Lars Built exists to make life more artful. As one of the top lifestyle blogs, they inspire soulful, hands-on living through everything from crafts to interior DIYs. With an ever-growing audience, their website needed to be as delightful as their projects and robust enough to keep up.

Scope

  • User Experience
  • Content Strategy
  • Web Design
Website Link

Design Role

As part of the Shoppe Theory team, I collaborated with Do-Hee Kim on a complete site redesign, translating the brand’s whimsical, maximalist aesthetic online. The challenge was balancing their big personality with easy navigation, so bold colors and quirky details never get in the way of finding what you came for.

Scallops, decorative borders, and color blocking add charm at every turn, while a clear structure keeps it all functional. In the words of the Lars team, the result feels “like the digital version of the most organized crafting closet,” brimming with surprises yet simple to explore.
