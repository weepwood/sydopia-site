Why should I work with you?

I’m equal parts left- and right-brained, meaning I pair the creative instinct to make something beautiful with the strategic thinking to make it work.

I’m also a Midwesterner, so I roll up my sleeves, do what I say I’ll do, and communicate clearly along the way. You’ll never need to chase deadlines or deliverables from me. I got you.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Brand identities and visual systems, websites, and UX for growing companies, from one-off projects to ongoing creative direction. I’m happiest when I can shape the brand and the digital experience together. Like my work but not sure where to start? Book a free intro call and we can talk it out :)

Do you offer retainers?

Yes, and I love working this way! Retainers give me room to get into the weeds and do deep creative strategy work. You get a dedicated block of my time each month, and I work as an embedded part of your team, from big-picture thinking to hands-on design. Most recently, I was Fractional Brand Director at OurFamilyWizard.

It’s ideal for growing startups or companies that need more creative support but aren’t ready to hire full time. Think ongoing brand and web work, a steady stream of UX projects, or creative direction as you scale. The goal is to give your creative a steady hand and a clear point of view.

Do you work with agencies or directly with brands?

Both. I’ve worked with studios like Shoppe Theory, Exygy, The Stable, and SMAKK, and I also work directly with founders and in-house teams. I like to do good work with nice people. Sounds like you? Hmu.

Do you work internationally?

Yes! I’m American and spent most of my career working with brands coast to coast, first from Minneapolis and Seattle, now from Berlin. Today I work with clients across the US and Europe.

What time zone are you in, & how do you handle the overlap?

I’m based in Berlin (CET). I’ve been working remotely since before the pandemic, so this is second nature to me. My afternoons line up with US mornings, and I’m happy to take evening calls to overlap with the rest of the US workday. Everything else happens asynchronously (I love a good Loom video), so projects keep moving while you sleep.

Do you build websites, or design only?

I design websites in Figma and can build them in Webflow when it’s the right fit. For more complex builds, I partner closely with developers to make sure the design comes to life exactly as intended.

Are you available for mentorship?

Yes! You can book a 1-on-1 meeting with me. Whether you want feedback on a personal project, need to talk through where you are in your career, or are ready to take the leap into freelance, I’d love to hear about it and help you figure out the path forward. I’m a no-bullshit advice giver who values actionable steps. It’s free (for now!), so get it while it’s hot.

How can we get started?

Book an intro call, or email me at hello@sydopia.com with a little about your project. We’ll talk through your goals, timeline, and whether we’re a good fit, and I’ll follow up with next steps.
